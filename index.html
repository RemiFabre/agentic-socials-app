agentic-socials

A personal YouTube analytics tool, made and used by Rémi Fabre for his own channel.

What it does

agentic-socials reads the statistics of the YouTube channel its owner authorizes, and turns them into private reports on his own computer: views, watch time, impressions and click-through rate, traffic sources, subscribers, and audience totals (countries, age groups, devices). Its only user is Rémi Fabre. It is not offered to the public.

What it asks Google for, and why

To read a channel's statistics, Google needs the channel owner's permission. agentic-socials asks for read-only access only:

  • "See your YouTube account": to check which channel was authorized.
  • "View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content": to read the statistics.

It cannot upload, edit, delete, comment, like or change anything on YouTube or in a Google account.

agentic-socials uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
