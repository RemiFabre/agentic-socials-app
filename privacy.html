Privacy policy for agentic-socials

Last updated: 31 October 2026

agentic-socials is a personal tool by Rémi Fabre that reads the statistics of his own YouTube channel to write private reports. It uses YouTube API Services. The Google Privacy Policy covers the data Google holds, and the YouTube Terms of Service cover the use of YouTube.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, and read-only:

  • the channel's id and name;
  • YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting statistics for the channel and its videos: views, watch time, retention, impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources, subscribers gained and lost, and audience totals by country, age group, gender and device;
  • if the owner turns it on, the channel's estimated revenue.

These statistics are totals: they hold no names or personal details of viewers. agentic-socials does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How it is used

Only to write reports and recommendations about the channel, for its owner.

Where it is stored

On the owner's own computer: the access key Google issues, and the statistics, in files only the owner's account can read. agentic-socials has no server, no user accounts, no cookies, no tracking and no ads. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (GitHub Privacy Statement).

Who sees it

The owner. The statistics are also read by an AI assistant, Claude by Anthropic, to write the reports. They are not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

As long as it is useful for the reports. agentic-socials checks at least every 30 days that its access is still authorized. If access is revoked, the data received through YouTube API Services is deleted within 30 days. A deletion request is carried out within 7 days.

How to revoke access

At any time, on the Google security settings page.

Google user data

agentic-socials' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/RemiFabre/agentic-socials-app/issues.

Any change to this policy is posted on this page with a new date.
